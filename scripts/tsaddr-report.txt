WAFFLEPOST TRUCK STOP ADDRESSES - v4.15.0, extended v4.16.0

v4.16.0: PROMOTED TO FULL ADDRESSES - street, city, state, ZIP.
v4.15.0 stored a street line and argued the stop card's own heading already
carried city and state. True on the card, and irrelevant everywhere the
address actually gets used - read aloud to a dispatcher, pasted into a nav
app, copied out of the share text.

The locality was NOT appended from the row. This atlas's `city` is the EXIT's
name, not a postal city, and the Waffle House field already proves they
diverge: Brooks KY files as Shepherdsville, Baldwin FL as Jacksonville, Good
Hope AL as Cullman, and a truck stop can differ again. Each street line was
geocoded on its own, biased to its Waffle House but not bounded by it, and
accepted only at house-number precision, in the row's own state, within 500 ft
of the distance that row's `feet` claims. 69 of 70 addresses - 66 rows plus 4
alternate stops - resolved on the first pass.

ZIP is the five-digit form. HERE returns ZIP+4 for most rooftops; the trailing
four are a delivery-route code nobody reads aloud.

STREET SPELLINGS WERE KEPT AS THE ATLAS HAS THEM. Fourteen differ from HERE's
canonical form - "1365 US-42" against "1365 US Highway 42 NE", "100 N Carter
Rd" against "100 Carter Rd N". The street lines came from the three-source
audit below; rewriting them to a geocoder's spelling would be unverified
editing dressed up as normalisation, and the geocode already proved both forms
resolve to the same building.

ONE STREET LINE DID CHANGE. Columbia TN carried "Bear Creek Pike" with no
house number, so nothing could resolve its locality - the bare street name
geocodes to a centroid 18,472 ft away. "1624 Bear Creek Pike" geocodes to
EXACTLY the audited 1,052 ft, delta 0, which is the same test that corroborated
the other 36 addresses. Recorded here rather than done silently, because the
rest of this pass deliberately changes no street lines.

The three HELD rows below are still held: an address that contradicts its own
row does not become shippable by gaining a city.

v4.15.0 - HOW THE STREET LINES WERE ESTABLISHED

40 rows had no truck stop address. 37 now do. 3 deliberately do not.

WHY THREE CHECKS AND NOT ONE.
The obvious method - ask HERE for the operator near the Waffle House and
take the nearest hit - was scored first against the 29 rows that already
carried a hand-audited address. Unbounded it agreed on 12 of 29 and would
have written eight or nine wrong addresses, mostly the same brand in
another state. Bounded to a circle around the Waffle House and arbitrated
by the audited feet it reached 23 of 29 at house-number precision - 87% -
and tightening the distance gate did not move that number, because the
remaining errors are near-neighbours on the same street that no distance
test can separate. One in eight wrong is the failure the 09-2026 re-audit
purged twelve rows for, so one source was not enough.

  A  bounded HERE place search, scored against the audited feet
  B  web research that never saw A, cited to a source per stop
  C  geocode of the agreed line, measured back to the Waffle House

AGREED  = A and B gave the same house number independently.
RESOLVED= they differed or A had nothing; settled against a named source.
HELD    = an address was found and it contradicts the row it belongs to.
